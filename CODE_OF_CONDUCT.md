# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the **Research Engineering OS** community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- ✅ **Being respectful** of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- ✅ **Giving and gracefully accepting** constructive feedback
- ✅ **Assuming good intent** when interpreting others' contributions and feedback
- ✅ **Demonstrating empathy and kindness** toward other community members
- ✅ **Welcoming newcomers** and helping them get started (especially students and early-career researchers)
- ✅ **Focusing on what is best** not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community
- ✅ **Acknowledging and learning from mistakes**, and taking responsibility for our actions
- ✅ **Using inclusive language** (e.g., "they" instead of gendered pronouns when unsure)
- ✅ **Respecting cultural and linguistic diversity** (this project supports 中文/English/日本語)

### Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- ❌ The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- ❌ Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- ❌ Public or private harassment
- ❌ Publishing others' private information (e.g., email addresses, physical addresses) without their explicit permission
- ❌ Dismissing or attacking someone based on their level of experience (e.g., "That's a stupid question")
- ❌ Gatekeeping (e.g., "You're not a real researcher/engineer if...")
- ❌ Making assumptions about someone's technical knowledge based on identity
- ❌ Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or educational setting

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies:

1. **Within project spaces** (GitHub repositories, Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, Wiki)
2. **In public spaces** when representing the project or community (e.g., social media, conferences, meetups)
3. **In private communications** related to project business (e.g., emails to maintainers)

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders (maintainers and moderators) are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will:

- Take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful
- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct
- Communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please report it:

### 📧 Email Report (Preferred for serious issues)
**Contact**: [Project Maintainer Email] (to be added)
- Reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality
- You will receive an acknowledgment within 48 hours
- We will work with you to understand the situation and determine next steps

### 🐛 GitHub Issues (For less serious concerns)
- Use the [Issue template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/config.yml)
- Mark as "Community Guidelines" concern
- Maintainers will review and respond

### ⚖️ What to include in your report:
- Your contact information (so we can follow up)
- Names (real, usernames, or pseudonyms) of any individuals involved
- Description of what happened (with links to relevant Issues/PRs if applicable)
- Any additional context that may be helpful
- Whether you'd like to remain anonymous to the accused party

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these **Community Impact Guidelines** to determine consequences:

### 1. Correction
**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other unprofessional behavior.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning providing clarity around the violation and why it was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning
**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. This includes:
- No interaction with involved parties (including unsolicited contact) for a specified period
- Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban

### 3. Temporary Ban
**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period. No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban
**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment, or aggression.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from all public interaction within the community.

## 📝 简体中文版 / Simplified Chinese Version

> 为确保包容性，我们提供中文摘要。完整条款以英文为准。

### 我们的承诺
我们承诺让 **Research Engineering OS** 社区成为一个无骚扰的参与环境，无论年龄、身材、残疾、种族、性别特征、性别认同、经验水平、教育背景、社会经济地位、国籍、个人外表、种族、宗教或性取向如何。

### 可接受的行为
- ✅ 尊重不同意见、观点和经验
- ✅ 给予和优雅接受建设性反馈
- ✅ 假定善意（在解读他人贡献时）
- ✅ 对社区成员展示同理心和善意
- ✅ 欢迎新人并帮助他们上手

### 不可接受的行为
- ❌ 使用性化语言或图像
- ❌ 辱骂、嘲讽或人身攻击
- ❌ 公开或私下骚扰
- ❌ 未经许可发布他人隐私信息
- ❌ 基于经验水平贬低他人（如"这是个愚蠢的问题"）

### 举报违规
如遇不当行为，请通过以下方式举报：
- 📧 邮件联系维护者（48 小时内回复）
- 🐛 GitHub Issues（标记为"Community Guidelines"）

举报将被谨慎和保密处理。

<div class="manga-story-label">Disaster of a Chain Reaction</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">At last, the most terrifying moment arrived. On the morning that had originally been scheduled for submitting the final version, a tiny change triggered a catastrophic chain reaction. Error messages surged like a tide, and the data she had painstakingly run for a whole week couldn’t be aligned due to environment drift. Rina slumped, clutching her head; the red circle marking the deadline on the wall was like a mocking eye, coldly watching her total defeat.</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">Exploring the Explanation of Exploration Debt</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">The book says: 'Exploration debt is the code and process debt incurred to quickly validate ideas. You think you're moving fast, but you're actually accumulating future costs.' Xiaoyan forced a bitter smile. Isn’t that describing her? Her code was full of TODOs, FIXMEs, “let’s just get it running for now”… The more excited she was back then about how fast the progress was, the more she regretted it now.</p>

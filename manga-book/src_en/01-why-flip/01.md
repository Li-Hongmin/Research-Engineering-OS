<div class="manga-story-label">A Book in the Morning Sun</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Day broke. Xiaoyan sat by the laboratory window and opened the first chapter of "Research Engineering OS." The very first sentence in the book struck her: 'Why is it that some code can never be finished refactoring? Why is it that some experiments can never be reproduced? The answer is that, in the early days of rapid progress, we buried far too much debt.' She gripped the book tightly. Maybe... the answer was right here.</p>

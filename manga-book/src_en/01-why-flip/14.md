<div class="manga-story-label">The Reviewers’ Doubts</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Devastating doubts arrived. Every line of the reviewers’ comments was pale yet forceful: “The results cannot be reproduced; the statistical significance is questionable.” Xiao Yan stared at the paper covered in red X’s, feeling all the strength drain from her body. That enormous verification monster had finally revealed its ferocious true form.</p>

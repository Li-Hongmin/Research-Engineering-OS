<div class="manga-story-label">Former Confidence</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Xiao Yan’s mind flashed back to the scene from three months ago. The project kickoff meeting—the whiteboard covered in grand plans. How confident she had been then, slapping the table and saying: “Let’s quickly get the framework up first; we can add tests and documentation later. Right now, we need speed!” Now, looking at those few words, she suddenly realized: that sentence was the source of all her problems now.</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">Joining a Large Project</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Six months passed. Xiaoyan’s project became more and more stable. The code was clean, the logs were thorough, and the Git history was in good shape. Her mentor noticed her progress and decided to have her join a large collaborative project. That day, she excitedly opened the shared repository... then froze. The same functionality had five different implementations. Naming conventions switched between camelCase and snake_case. Some comments were in English, others in Chinese. Th-this... is this really a project?</p>

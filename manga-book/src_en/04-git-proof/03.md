<div class="manga-story-label">Redemption in the Timestamps</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">In that moment of despair, Xiaoyan’s eyes suddenly fell on the screen beside the desk—Git. Those commits. The commits she had been making all along, each with a timestamp. Her whole body stiffened at once, and then she slowly sat up straight. I... I’ve been keeping records. Every change has a timestamp. Every commit has an author signature. Every single line of code change has been recorded. “I have evidence,” she said softly. “I always have.”</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">Rollback Safety Net</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Xiaoyan saw a famous commit in her senior sister’s repository: “Fixed everything” (1247 files changed). Three months of work committed all at once, and the commit message was just four words. Later, when her senior sister needed to look back and figure out “when exactly did I change the data preprocessing,” she could only open that gigantic commit and stare blankly at the 1,200+ changes. “This is why I now make several small commits every day,” Xiaoyan said to herself.</p>

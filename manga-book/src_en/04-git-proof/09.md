<div class="manga-story-label">Tracing the Story</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Looking back at her own commit history, Rina noticed a key difference: some people compress three months of work into one huge commit, “fixed everything”; others make several carefully described small commits every day. The former? Totally like looking at flowers in the fog. The latter? That’s a clear story. “I want to be the latter.”</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">The Value of Time Stamps</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Every commit is like a complete court record. The hash is a fingerprint—unique; the timestamp is proof of time—non-repudiable; the author field shows who did it; and the commit message—that must be clear, it must be meaningful. Rina stared at the <code>git show</code> output on the screen and suddenly realized: “I’ve been unintentionally creating a perfect chain of evidence.”</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">Narrating the Commit History</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Tears blurred Xiaoyan’s eyes, but this time they were tears of relief. She looked at the entire timeline—every commit had a clear date, the author’s name, a hash value, and a well-written commit message. The standards she used to find tedious—organizing work by date, writing clear commit messages, keeping a regular commit cadence—now shone brilliantly, like evidence prepared specifically for this moment. “I didn’t just do this research honestly. I also have professional records proving I did. The reviewers will see it.”</p>

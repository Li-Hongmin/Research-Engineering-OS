<div class="manga-story-label">The Power of Git</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">She opened the terminal and typed <code>git log</code>. On the screen, a dense stream of commit records began to scroll. Xiaoyan’s eyes followed every line: Jan 15—“Add initial data exploration script,” Jan 22—“Fix data outlier detection,” Feb 3—“Implement baseline model”... The entire three-month research journey was right here, precise down to every single change. Every timestamp was saying: this isn’t made up, this isn’t hindsight. This really happened.</p>

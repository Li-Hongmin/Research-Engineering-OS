<div class="manga-story-label">Integrity of the Chain</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Xiaoyan began to learn how to write clear commit messages. Not "bug fix," but "Fix data preprocessing: remove outliers >3σ." Not "update," but "Add baseline LSTM model with 84.2% validation accuracy." Each message was like a diary entry, telling future readers (who might also be reviewers): "At this step, what am I doing, and why am I doing it."</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">The Lesson of Blame</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">A classmate, Amin, once had a painfully bitter experience. He spent a week tuning model parameters and finally achieved 94% accuracy. He ran validation locally and was ecstatic watching the results. But he didn’t submit. A few days later, his computer had issues and he reinstalled the system. That 94% model... was gone. He hugged his computer and burst into tears. From then on, he understood a truth: “Work without a commit, in the computer’s eyes, doesn’t exist.” After hearing this story, Rina made up her mind: every valuable change must be committed immediately.</p>

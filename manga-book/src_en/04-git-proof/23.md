<div class="manga-story-label">Building Trust</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Xiao Yan shut down the computer and walked over to the window. That email questioning her honesty had now become her strongest evidence. She realized a profound shift: before, she was afraid of being questioned because she had no proof. Now, she had Git. It doesn’t lie. It doesn’t forget. Every timestamp, every change, every commit message—they’re all there, tamper-proof and undeniable. “Git,” she murmured, “is like the best lawyer a researcher could have.”</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">But Xiaoyan suddenly thought of a new question. With Git’s perfect record, can I say my work is “done”? At what moment can I truly, confidently commit my code or submit my paper and say, “This is the final version”? The answer lies in something that seems simple but is often overlooked: the definition of done. In the next chapter, what Xiaoyan will learn is how to clearly define “what counts as done,” so she won’t fall into endless improvement.</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">Completion of the Proof</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">She decided to take action immediately. She opened git log and reviewed her commits from the past week. Were the commit messages clear? Were the changes a reasonable size? Was the frequency consistent? She found a few areas to improve—one commit changed too many files, and another message wasn’t specific enough. “From now on,” she told herself, “I’ll treat every commit as seriously as writing a paper. This isn’t just for my own workflow, but also for anyone who may need to review my work in the future.”</p>

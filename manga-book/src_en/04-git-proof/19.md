<div class="manga-story-label">The Art of Rebase</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">The best part is: when a reviewer says, "I want to see the model you had when you made the final submission," Xiao Yan can reply directly, "I’ll give you the checkout command, and you can get exactly the same code and model." Not "As far as I remember…," but "This is that exact version." That kind of confidence and precision will turn a reviewer’s skepticism into trust.</p>

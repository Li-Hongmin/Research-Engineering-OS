<div class="manga-story-label">Peace After Submission</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">In the two months after submission, Rina’s days became exceptionally peaceful. She could finally breathe a sigh of relief. Three years of data collection, countless late nights tweaking parameters, polishing the paper from the first draft to the tenth version... it was all over. Now, the paper was in the reviewers’ hands, and she had done everything she could. She even began to look forward to their response—she was confident in her results. After all, she had a complete history in her Git repository, with every experimental step clearly documented. That was the proof.</p>
</div>

<div class="manga-story">
<p class="manga-story-content">However, this morning, the email’s subject line read like a court verdict. “Reviewer Concern: Please provide timestamped evidence that experiments were conducted before accessing test data”—Rina stared at that line, and it felt as if the whole world had turned black and white. The reviewer was questioning her honesty. Three years of hard work, those sleepless nights, all the theoretical validations... could it all be reduced to nothing in the face of this single question mark?</p>
</div>

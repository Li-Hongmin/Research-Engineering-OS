<div class="manga-story-label">A Year in Review</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">She closed her eyes and recalled that desperate night a year ago. Overwhelmed by three monsters, unable to see where to go, she had even thought about giving up. She remembered that suffocating feeling, that “it’s over, it’s all over” despair. But at that moment, her mentor handed her that book. And she decided that, in the remaining 72 hours, she would change everything.</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">The Temptation of ChatGPT</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">After the logging system was improved, Xiaoyan encountered her first temptation of the AI era. One afternoon, she heard that ChatGPT could generate code. With a “let’s give it a try” mindset, she typed in a question: 'Write me a function to process CSV files.' In less than three seconds, a complete, nicely formatted function appeared on the screen. Xiaoyan could hardly believe it—'How is this possible? So fast!' Without thinking much, she copied and pasted it directly into the project.</p>

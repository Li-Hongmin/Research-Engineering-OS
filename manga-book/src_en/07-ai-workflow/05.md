<div class="manga-story-label">Understanding Comes Before Use</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">"The key in the AI era is not how to use AI to generate code, but how to use AI responsibly." The mentor gave her a piece of advice. First, clearly define requirements and constraints. Instead of vaguely saying, "Write me a function," describe it in detail: "What data should this function handle, what format should it return, what performance requirements must it meet, and what edge cases does it need to handle?"</p>

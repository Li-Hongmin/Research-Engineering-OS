<p class="manga-story-content">A week later, the scene was completely different. Xiaoyan sat in front of her computer, staring at a screen full of error messages. She wanted to modify the AI-written code to fix the bug, but found that she couldn’t understand the logic of this code at all. Why was this kind of data structure used here? Why were edge cases handled like that over there? She tried to read it line by line and grew more and more confused. Worst of all, this was her own project—she should have understood every line of code in it, but now she felt powerless, like an outsider.</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">Code You Can’t Understand</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">The mentor walked past her desk and saw her struggling. “What’s wrong?” the mentor asked. Xiaoyan pointed at the screen and said, “The AI-generated code has a bug. I want to fix it, but I can’t understand it at all.” The mentor paused for a moment, then said in a calm but firm tone, “That’s the problem. AI can write code, but it can’t understand the code for you. When something goes wrong and you need to fix it, but you can’t understand it—the responsibility is still on you.” Xiaoyan suddenly understood: she wasn’t saving time; she was taking a risk.</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">The Necessity of Code Review</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Xiaoyan found that AI is very good at assisting with documentation writing. She used AI to draft the documentation for a module, then reviewed, corrected, and supplemented it manually. Efficiency tripled, and quality did not decline. But the key was that she always retained the right to review the content.</p>

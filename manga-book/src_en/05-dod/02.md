<div class="manga-story-label">The Advisor’s First Doubt</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Fifteen minutes after the email was sent, the advisor’s reply arrived.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"Thanks for your hard work. Could you reproduce the results?"</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">A brief sentence, yet it instantly cooled Rina’s excitement. She frowned.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"What? Could numbers lie?"</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">She quickly reran the entire experimental procedure. But when she clicked Run again, she began to feel a little uneasy. The progress bar on the screen crept forward...</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">An hour passed. The model was still training.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"Wait, is this setup... the same as before?"</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 Why do I have this uneasy feeling in my heart? Just one sentence from my mentor made me start doubting my work.</em></p>

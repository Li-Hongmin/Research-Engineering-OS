<div class="manga-story-label">Landmine 1 Explodes - Cannot Be Reproduced</div>
<p class="manga-story-content"><strong>The afternoon of the second day after rerunning the experiment.</strong></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Rina stared at the screen, her fingers lightly tapping on the keyboard. Again and again, she compared the code: the parameters were the same, the dataset was the same, and even the random seed was the same...</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">But the number that came out was different.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">The first two times were both +0.89. Now it’s +0.71.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Her whole body stiffened instantly.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"Th-This... how is that possible?"</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">She rushed back to the experiment folder and pulled up the previous logs. Back then, she had hurriedly gone through them once to meet the group meeting deadline. Now, taking a closer look, she realized she had modified a configuration file at the time but forgot to record it.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 What kind of experiment am I even running? It’s clearly the same code, yet the results are different. How can I prove this is real progress, and not just a stroke of luck?</em></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Her advisor’s email flashed into her mind again: “Can you reproduce it?”</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 Right now, I can’t answer “yes.”</em></p>

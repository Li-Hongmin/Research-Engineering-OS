<div class="manga-story-label">10-Minute Action - Write Down Your DoD</div>
<p class="manga-story-content"><strong>What is your DoD?</strong></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">In Xiaoyan’s story, she learned:</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">✓ Reproducibility: Can I reproduce this result again in any environment?</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">✓ Comprehensibility: Do I know why each decision exists, and what will happen if I change it?</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">✓ Fairness: Have I compared against the true standard baseline?</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">✓ Auditability: Are my code and logs clear enough for others (or my future self) to understand?</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><strong>10-Minute Action:</strong></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Open the experiment you’re currently working on. Write down your definition of “done”—not “the code runs,” but the moment when “I can take responsibility for it.”</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">It can be as simple as:</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"The signs that my data analysis is complete are: (1) all charts have error bars, (2) I can explain every outlier, and (3) the code can be rerun without changing any parameters."</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 With a DoD, “done” changes from a vague feeling into a verifiable fact.</em></p>

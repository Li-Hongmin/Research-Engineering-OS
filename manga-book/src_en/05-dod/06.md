<div class="manga-story-label">Three Invisible Landmines</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">In the email her advisor sent her, three issues were listed one by one—like three hidden landmines buried in the words “more or less.”</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><strong>Landmine #1: Can I reproduce it?</strong></p>
<p class="manga-story-content">Every run produced different results. Only then did she realize she had never set a fixed random seed. That 0.89 result might have just been good luck.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><strong>Landmine 2: Why does this work?</strong></p>
<p class="manga-story-content">The advisor asked: Which hyperparameter did you change that made the result drop back to 0.65? She had no answer. She hadn’t done any ablation experiments at all.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><strong>Landmine 3: Is there a fair baseline?</strong></p>
<p class="manga-story-content">She used a baseline she wrote herself, rather than an industry-standard method. So is her algorithm actually better, or is her baseline just too weak?</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 I thought “done” meant the code runs and the numbers look good... but every question my advisor asked hit a blind spot I’d never considered.</em></p>

<div class="manga-story-label">Three Deadly Questions</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Xiaoyan still hadn’t recovered from the blow of the failed replication when the advisor’s third email arrived.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"By the way, did you do a fair comparison with the baseline? Are you using the same version of the library?"</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Xiaoyan’s hands began to tremble. She looked back over her notes.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">The baseline uses an older version of TensorFlow. Her method uses a newer version. The newer version has improvements in GPU optimization, so the baseline might have been slower to begin with...</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">She had never thought of that. She had always believed her improvements were so successful, but it turned out that part of it might have been simply due to differences in the library version.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 So... what exactly am I proving? That my approach is better, or just that I used a newer library?</em></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">She opened the email, intending to write a reply to her advisor. But her fingers on the keyboard froze once again.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 I... I can’t really explain it clearly.</em></p>

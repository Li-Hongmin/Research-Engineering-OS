<div class="manga-story-label">Landmine 2 Explosion - Not Sure Why It Works</div>
<p class="manga-story-content"><strong>After Rina finally reproduced the experiment, she decided to dig deeper into the reasons behind the results.</strong></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Where exactly did this +0.89 improvement come from—those three new lines of code she added? The changes to data preprocessing? Or was it just pure luck?</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">She began running ablation experiments—deleting the code line by line and observing how the results changed.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">First deletion: still +0.89.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Second deletion: still +0.89.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">The third time, I deleted everything: the result became +0.88.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">She stared blankly at the screen.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"Wait... then what exactly is the code I added... doing?"</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">She reviewed her own implementation once. There were very few comments. Some of the variable names were obscure. Even she could barely understand it.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 Can I explain why changing the code this way improves accuracy? Can I explain it to my advisor or reviewers? If I can’t explain it clearly, then my improvement is a black box. A black box can’t be trusted.</em></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">She opened the email, wanting to write her advisor a detailed explanation. But her fingers on the keyboard froze once again.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 I... I actually can’t really explain why changing it like this would work.</em></p>

<div class="manga-story-label">The Most Critical Moment</div>
<p class="manga-story-content"><strong>That night, Xiaoyan sat in the office; it was already late.</strong></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Her mind was in a complete mess. Three landmines. Three questions. All of them were fatal.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Cannot be reproduced. Cannot be explained. Unfair comparison.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">These problems didn’t just appear in her research alone. What about other labs? The entire discipline? Is this a widespread problem?</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">She thought of her mentor’s words. “Can you reproduce it?” Those three words carried a heavy sense of responsibility.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 What is the point of our research, really? If the results can’t be reproduced, if I can’t explain why, if the comparison itself isn’t fair... then when I publish this paper, who am I deceiving? The reviewers? The editor? The readers? Or myself?</em></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">She opened a blank document, ready to write an email to her advisor.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"Mentor, I’ve realized there are serious problems with my work. I need to start over. But I don’t know what I should do..."</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Her hands lingered on the keyboard for a long time.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 If I admit these problems, then my three months of work will be completely overturned. But if I don’t admit them... that would be deceiving myself.</em></p>

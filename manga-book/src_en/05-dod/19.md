<p class="manga-story-content"><strong>Benefit 1: Confidently say "Done"</strong></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">At the paper discussion meeting a week later, it was Rina’s turn to present.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">She opened the slide deck; it was no longer “I tuned a model, and the accuracy is 0.89,” but rather:</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"My algorithm outperforms the SOTA baseline by 4% on all three benchmark datasets. Ablation experiments show that the key contributions come from feature engineering (60%) and model architecture adjustments (30%). I have released fully reproducible code on GitHub."</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">The advisor nodded. The other students asked some detailed questions, and Rina answered them one by one—because she now knew the answers.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 It’s no longer that kind of “I finished it, but deep down I’m not really sure” feeling. Now, I can stand behind every single sentence.</em></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">After the meeting, a senior PhD student approached her: “Your presentation style was different this time—especially clear. Can you teach me how to organize my experiments?”</p>

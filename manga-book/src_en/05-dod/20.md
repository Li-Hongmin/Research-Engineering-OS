<p class="manga-story-content"><strong>Benefit 2: Reviewers Can’t Attack Your Work</strong></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Three months later, the first paper Rina had submitted came back with the reviewers’ comments.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Reviewer R2 raised a concern: "Your ablation study is not comprehensive enough and does not demonstrate that the architectural modifications to the model are necessary."</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">But this time, Rina was no longer flustered. She opened her lab notebook and found the corresponding data:</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"I did indeed run this experiment. After removing the architectural changes, the accuracy dropped from 0.89 to 0.81. The additional comparative experiments will be included in the revised version."</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 In the past, I would think: "Oh, I didn’t think of that." Now, I know every corner of my work.</em></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Her reply wasn’t an apology—it was evidence.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">In the end, the paper was accepted. In the final comments, the reviewer wrote: "Author's response demonstrates thorough experimental rigor."</p>

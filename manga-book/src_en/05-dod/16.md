<p class="manga-story-content"><strong>DoD Item 4: Ablation Experiments Completed</strong></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Xiaoyan sat back down in front of the computer. This time, she didn’t directly tweak all the hyperparameters.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">She wrote down a checklist:</p>
<p class="manga-story-content">- Remove feature A, accuracy → 0.84</p>
<p class="manga-story-content">- Remove feature B, accuracy → 0.79</p>
<p class="manga-story-content">- Simplify the number of model layers, accuracy → 0.71</p>
<p class="manga-story-content">- Adjust the learning rate to three different values, resulting in → 0.87, 0.89, 0.81 respectively</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 It turns out that every seemingly insignificant choice is affecting the final outcome. I have to know which decision 0.89 came from, and what would happen if I changed it.</em></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">After two days of systematic experiments, she finally understood: the most critical contribution came from Feature A, accounting for 60% of the performance improvement. And although the choice of learning rate affected the final numbers, its stability was very high.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"Now," she said to the log, "I really can explain my 0.89."</p>

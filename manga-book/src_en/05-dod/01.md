<div class="manga-story-label">Xiaoyan’s Excitement and Hesitation</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Xiaoyan took a deep breath and opened the email editor. She reread the output of that line of code three times—finally, the number settled at an accuracy of +0.89.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"It worked!" she shouted to herself. The days and nights of the past three months—tuning the model, cleaning the data, and testing over and over—had all been worth it.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">But at the moment she pressed the send button, she hesitated.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Her fingers hovered over the keyboard as she glanced out of the corner of her eye toward her advisor’s office. The image of her advisor’s aged face flashed through her mind—he always liked to ask those “odd” questions at thesis discussion meetings, tearing perfectly good research results to shreds with his probing.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"Forget it," she muttered. "I’ve checked everything. There shouldn’t be any problems this time."</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">I typed the email subject line: “Experiment Completion Report.”</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">She clicked send.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><strong>The mentor opened a document.</strong></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"I call this checklist the 'Definition of Done.' In other words, what counts as being 'truly finished.'"</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">He pointed at the few lines of text on the screen:</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">✓ Results are reproducible<br/>
✓ Can explain why it works<br/>
✓ Fair comparison with baselines<br/>
✓ Complete code and documentation<br/>
✓ Passed peer review</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"This isn’t some new invention. Many mature teams have been using similar things for a long time already," the mentor said. "But the key is that you need to truly understand the reason behind each one."</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">He looked at Xiaoyan. “You have a chance to learn this right now. Those three landmines you just stepped on happen to correspond exactly to the first three items on this list.”</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 Oh... so the setbacks I’m going through right now are actually the best learning material for DoD.</em></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"Now, let's discuss each item one by one." The mentor turned toward the whiteboard.</p>

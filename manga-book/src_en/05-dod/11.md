<div class="manga-story-label">Principles for Giving Spoiler Warnings in Advance</div>
<p class="manga-story-content"><strong>The mentor looked at the dejected student in front of him and let out a sigh.</strong></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"Xiaoyan, I know you're very disappointed right now. Three months of work seems to have all been overturned."</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"But in fact, the process you’re going through right now is more valuable than success."</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Xiaoyan raised her head, her eyes filled with confusion.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"Look," the mentor drew a few Xs on the whiteboard, "these are landmines: landmines that can’t be reproduced, landmines that can’t be explained, and landmines of unfair comparisons."</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"Where are these landmines most dangerous?" The advisor paused for a moment. "They usually explode when you publish a paper and gain recognition in academic circles."</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">"So, rather than waiting for the mines to explode, it’s better to clear them out from the start. Defuse them in advance."</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">He turned to face Xiaoyan. "You need a checklist. A checklist to make sure your work doesn’t have these fatal weaknesses."</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 Heads-up in advance... If that were the case, I might never have fallen into the predicament I’m in now.</em></p>

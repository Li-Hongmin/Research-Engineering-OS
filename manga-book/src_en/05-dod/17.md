<p class="manga-story-content"><strong>DoD Item 5: Code Reviewable</strong></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Now, she has organized the code. Instead of piling up ten versions in a messy local folder, she keeps clear branches and commits in Git.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Each commit message is very clear:</p>
<p class="manga-story-content">"Add a preprocessing step for feature A (performance +5%)"</p>
<p class="manga-story-content">"Change the learning rate from 0.01 to 0.001 to improve convergence stability"</p>
<p class="manga-story-content">"Integrate a publicly available baseline model for comparison"</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">She sent the code link to a classmate and asked him to do a quick review.</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Fifteen minutes later, he replied: “Huh, there’s a bug here—the index is out of bounds on line 23. But your ablation experiments are very clear; I can fully understand your line of thinking.”</p>

<p class="manga-story-content"><em>💭 Great! He found it before I even proactively noticed the problem. And he isn’t asking questions blindly—he really understands what I’m doing.</em></p>

<p class="manga-story-content">Xiaoyan quickly fixed that bug and submitted again. "This time, I really finished it."</p>

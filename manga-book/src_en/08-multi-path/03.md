<p class="manga-story-content">Seeing her agonize over it in the office for several days, her advisor walked over and asked, “What are you thinking about?” Xiaoyan explained the situation. After listening, the advisor smiled and said, “Why do you have to choose just one?” Xiaoyan froze. The advisor continued, “One of the purposes of Git branches is to let you explore multiple paths at the same time. On different branches, you can try different ideas, and then decide which one is better in the end.” Xiaoyan’s eyes lit up instantly—right, why bet on only one direction? I can try them all!</p>

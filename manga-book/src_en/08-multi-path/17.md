<p class="manga-story-content">"Don’t delete those failed branches," the mentor said. Xiaoyan kept experiment-A and experiment-B. In the future, if she needed to look back on why she didn’t choose A or B, these branches would be living evidence. And perhaps, one day, there would be a new idea that would need to use some part of A or B.</p>

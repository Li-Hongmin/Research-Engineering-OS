<p class="manga-story-content">The previous lesson was still deeply etched in her heart. Once, she stuck to a single direction and wrote code for a full two weeks, only to realize at the very end that this path wouldn’t work—the algorithm itself had fundamental limitations. All the work was wasted, and she had to start over from scratch. She didn’t want to go through that feeling of failure and waste again. Now, she had become cautious, even a bit hesitant.</p>

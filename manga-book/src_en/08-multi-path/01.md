<div class="manga-story-label">A Choice Between Two Directions</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">After learning the guidelines for AI collaboration, Xiaoyan faced a new research challenge. She had two possible research directions in front of her—Method A looked concise, and Method B seemed more powerful. But which one should she choose? She stared at the two experimental plans, caught in deep indecision. Because she knew that if she chose the wrong direction, she might waste several weeks. Once she committed, the cost of changing direction would be too high.</p>

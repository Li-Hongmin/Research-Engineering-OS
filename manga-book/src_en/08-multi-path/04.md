<div class="manga-story-label">The Power of Method B</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">The mentor taught her a clear branching strategy. The main branch always remains stable and runnable; only the best, verified solution can be merged into main. Experimental branches, on the other hand, are free, temporary, and can be discarded at any time. In this way, the goals of stability and exploration are completely separated and won’t interfere with each other.</p>

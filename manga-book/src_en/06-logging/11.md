<div class="manga-story-label">The First Logging Framework</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Her mentor suggested she use a structured logging format—JSON. That way, computers can parse these logs more easily, and statistical analysis becomes more convenient too. Instead of plain-text “something happened,” it would be JSON like {'timestamp': '2024-01-15T10:30:45', 'level': 'INFO', 'module': 'data_loader', 'message': 'Load complete', 'records': 1024}.</p>

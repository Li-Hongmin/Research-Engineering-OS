<div class="manga-story-label">Convenience of Search</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Xiao Yan shares her logging experience with a newly joined junior. “Logging isn’t just for debugging,” she says. “It’s also for understanding—understanding what your program is doing and where the problem lies. And it’s also for proving—proving what your process was like, what decisions you made, and how the results were obtained. A project without logs is like a secret with no traces; no one believes it.”</p>

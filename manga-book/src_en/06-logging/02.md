<div class="manga-story-label">A Screen Full of print Statements</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Xiao Yan turned around and looked at the code from the past three months. print statements were everywhere. 'print(data)', 'print("here")', 'print(result)'... Back then, to debug quickly, she added these statements as she wrote. At the time, it didn’t seem like a big deal—seeing the output was enough. But now, it had become a disaster. Every time the program ran, the screen was a mess. Where was the information she needed? Who knows.</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">Effective Information in INFO</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Xiaoyan defined five levels for logs: DEBUG (the most detailed debugging information), INFO (important business processes), WARNING (a warning, but still continuing), ERROR (an error occurred), and CRITICAL (a fatal error). Different levels use different icons and colors, just like traffic lights—you can understand them at a glance.</p>

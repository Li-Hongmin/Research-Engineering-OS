<div class="manga-story-label">Performance Optimization</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Three months later, a concurrency issue troubled Rina for an entire day. Multiple processes were competing for a resource, causing unstable results. She opened the log analysis tool and searched by experiment ID, and immediately saw which process acquired the resource at what moment, which process got stuck, and in what order they ran. Within five minutes, the root cause surfaced. She suddenly realized that a good logging system is like installing surveillance cameras on your code.</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">Log Chaos</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Thousands of lines of logs were jumbled together. Some were debug messages, some were intermediate results, some were meaningless “here,” and some she had no idea when they were printed. Rina stared at the screen, her eyes scrolling downward nonstop, yet she still couldn’t find the one log entry she needed. Her head began to ache, and a wave of helplessness washed over her. The information she needed was like a needle buried in sand—and there was endless sand.</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">What Is a Good Log</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Once again, she went to her mentor for help. The mentor glanced at the log, then opened an example of a structured log. “Look at this,” the mentor said. Each log entry clearly labeled the level (DEBUG, INFO, WARNING, ERROR), timestamp, module name, and contextual information. Different kinds of information were highlighted in different colors. The entire log file was like a clear ledger, not a pile of garbage.</p>

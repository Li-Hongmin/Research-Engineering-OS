<div class="manga-story-label">The Mentor’s Rescue Appears</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">At the very moment despair was about to swallow her, the laboratory door was gently pushed open. A beam of light shot in, and the monsters began to shriek as they retreated. Xiaoyan lifted her head—the mentor stood in the doorway, holding a glowing book. The mentor said calmly: 'I think you need to understand one thing. About how to solve these problems systematically.'</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">The Ultimate Moment of Despair</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">In the end, the three monsters closed in. Xiaoyan collapsed to her knees, pinned immobile by the invisible giant hands of exploration debt, validation debt, and reproduction debt. 72 hours. She raised her head to look at the computer—still those flashing red error messages. Her eyes were hollow and despairing. The brief burst of rapid progress had now turned into an endless predicament. She couldn’t think of a way out.</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">The Light Drove Away the Monsters</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Xiaoyan reached out with trembling hands to take the book. A warm golden light illuminated her face. The despair in her eyes began to fade, replaced by a faint glimmer of hope. Behind her, the three monsters began to shriek and retreat—as if driven back by the beam of light. Maybe... maybe there was still a way.</p>

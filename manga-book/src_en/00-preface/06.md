<p class="manga-story-content">Just then, the dark corner began to writhe. Three enormous figures emerged from the shadows—tangled code and TODO comments (exploration debt), shattered testing equipment and missing validation (validation debt), scattered configuration files and version chaos (reproducibility debt). Xiao Yan watched them slowly take shape and suddenly understood: these... were all mines I had planted myself.</p>

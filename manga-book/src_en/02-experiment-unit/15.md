<div class="manga-story-label">Establishing the Logging System</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Rina gave a wry smile. Three months ago, she had gotten the code running on her own laptop and excitedly ran it on the lab’s server—only for it to throw an error immediately. A bunch of library versions didn’t match, and the Python version was wrong too. “It clearly runs on my computer!” she freaked out at the screen. This was the real-life version of that famous programmer’s verse—my computer and your computer are, evidently, home to different gods.</p>

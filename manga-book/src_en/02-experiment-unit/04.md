<div class="manga-story-label">The Story of Parameters</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">'Imagine a living biological cell,' the mentor said. 'Code is like the nucleus, but a nucleus alone can't function. You need the cell membrane, mitochondria, all kinds of organelles—they only form a life capable of survival and reproduction when they work together. An experiment is like such a cell.' Rina suddenly understood: before, they had merely piled a bunch of organelles together at random—no wonder nothing worked.</p>

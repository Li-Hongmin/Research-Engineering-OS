<div class="manga-story-label">Reproducing the Experiment</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Rina remembered what happened a month ago. She had gotten great results on a dataset and excitedly told her classmates. But a month later, when a classmate repeated her experiment, they found the results were completely different. Only later did they discover that the data had been accidentally modified once—someone had deleted some bad data and then forgot to tell her. At that moment, Rina understood: no matter how good you are, if the data changes, the results are worthless.</p>

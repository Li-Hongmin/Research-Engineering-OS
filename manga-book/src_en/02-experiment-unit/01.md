<p class="manga-story-content">After studying the previous chapter, Rina understood the root cause of debt. Now she couldn’t wait to start making changes—but when she opened the project folder, she was instantly dumbfounded. She stared at this tangled mess and thought, “How long is it going to take to sort this out?”</p>

<p class="manga-story-content">“The last two elements are the results and the logs.” The mentor pointed to the last two vertices. “In the end, an experiment produces outputs—models, data, visualization charts. All of these must be saved and bound to the experiment’s ID. In addition, the logs during the process are even more critical—this is the only evidence that allows others to understand how you arrived at this result.” Xiaoyan nodded. She was increasingly coming to understand that more important than the final result was that clear chain of reasoning.</p>

<div class="manga-story-label">Filename Conventions</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">"The data/ directory stores all data. But note," the advisor emphasized, "data is usually too large and shouldn’t be committed to Git. We put a .gitignore under the data/ directory to tell Git not to track these files." Xiaoyan wrote rules like *.csv and *.pkl in .gitignore. Data is like fresh groceries at home—you can’t leave it outside the freezer for too long—so it doesn’t go into the repository.</p>

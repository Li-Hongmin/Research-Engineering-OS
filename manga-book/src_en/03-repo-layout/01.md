<div class="manga-story-label">An Introduction to Folder Structure</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">After mastering the concept of an experimental unit, Rina began applying it to her own projects. But immediately, she ran into new trouble. Although she could now record the six elements of a single experiment, as the number of experiments grew and the codebase became more complex, she had no idea how to organize all of it. Opening the project root directory, she stared blankly at the screen. Some files were in the root, some in src, some in experiment, and each folder followed a different naming convention. It was so chaotic it didn’t look like a project at all—more like a junk heap.</p>

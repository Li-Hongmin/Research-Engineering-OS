<div class="manga-story-label">The Possibility of Collaboration</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Xiaoyan recalled a mistake she had made before. She put a model that hadn’t been fully validated directly into src/, thinking she could always change it later anyway. As a result, other parts of the code began to depend on that model, and when she tried to modify it, a whole bunch of places started breaking along with it. In that moment, she understood why fast and slow variables need to be separated—this is to protect reliable code from being overwhelmed by the turbulence of experimentation.</p>

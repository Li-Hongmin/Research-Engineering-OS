<div class="manga-story-label">From Individual to Team</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Xiaoyan marked a weekly “Code Graduation Day” on her calendar. On that day, she would sit down and carefully review the code in experiments/. What was stable enough already? What was still being iterated on? Anything that met the criteria would be selected, then—after testing and documentation improvements—moved into src/. This process made her feel like she was truly building an engineering project, rather than casually piling up code.</p>

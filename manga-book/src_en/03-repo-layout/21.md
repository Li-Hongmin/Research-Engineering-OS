<div class="manga-story-label">The Temptation of Version Control</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">Three months later, Xiaoyan opened the project again. The previous scene of densely packed folders was gone. Now, she immediately remembered: experimental code was in experiments, models were in src, and parameters were in configs. She even said a word of thanks to her past self—"Thank you, organized me; now I can get back into the groove so quickly." Time was saved, invisibly, just like that.</p>

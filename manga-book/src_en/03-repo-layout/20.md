<div class="manga-story-label">The Necessity of Git</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">The quickest validation came from a new team member. There were no questions like “Where is the file?” or “What is this used for?” For the first time, Rina felt that a good structure not only helps oneself, but is also a gift to others.</p>

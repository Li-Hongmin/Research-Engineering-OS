<div class="manga-story-label">The Third Crash</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">'Why is it always like this!' Rina slammed her fist on the table. This was the third time. The previous two times, it had also crashed right at the critical moment. Her mind began racing, trying to retrace her steps.</p>

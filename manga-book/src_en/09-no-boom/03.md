<div class="manga-story-label">Defensive Programming</div>
<p class="manga-story-content">The mentor walked in holding a cup of coffee. After glancing at the crash message on the screen, the mentor said calmly: “This time the problem isn’t that the code was written poorly, but that you didn’t defend. That’s what defensive programming is—trust nothing. Don’t trust that the input is correct, don’t trust that intermediate results are correct, and don’t trust that external resources even exist.” Xiaoyan looked up, a flicker of understanding passing through her eyes.</p>
